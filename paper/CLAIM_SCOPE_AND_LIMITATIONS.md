# Claim scope and limitations

## Proved

1. The displayed quartic sum of nine squares in ten variables has exactly
   `1152` distinct isolated real zeros.
2. Hence `tilde#(4,10) >= 1152 > 2^10`, refuting the universal
   Ottaviani--Shapiro formula `tilde#(2k,l)=k^l`.
3. The base ideal has the displayed real-radical decomposition.
4. Its complex coordinate ring is finite free of rank `512` over `C[t]` and
   is an unmixed one-dimensional complete intersection.
5. For every even `k>=2` and `l>=3`,

   ```text
   tilde#(2k,l) >= ((l-1)(k-1)/4) k^(l-1).
   ```

6. The largest even summand degree `e=2 floor(k/2)` gives the stated transfer
   for all `k>=2`, and strict violations multiply over disjoint blocks.

## Not proved

- no exact formula for the extremal function;
- no proof that `1152` is optimal at `(k,l)=(2,10)`;
- no matching upper bound for the general family;
- no classification of extremizers;
- no absolute historical-priority claim;
- no peer-review or proof-assistant-verification claim.

## Priority language

A documented search through 2026-08-09 found no earlier published or publicly
posted identical construction or theorem implying the stated family. This is
a qualified negative-search conclusion, subject to indexing, access,
terminology, language, and private-circulation limitations.
